Advanced Artificial Intelligence

The course aims to enrich students with quick Review of the main concepts of Artificial Intelligence, Description logics, Logical foundations of Ontology Engineering, Lexical Semantics, WordNet, Arabic Ontology, Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval.
